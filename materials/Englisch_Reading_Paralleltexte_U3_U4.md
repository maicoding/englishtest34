```text
Question word -> keyword -> text evidence -> answer
```

Jede Antwort braucht einen kleinen Beleg.

## Text 1: A different kind of camp

Mia did not want to spend two weeks at a nature camp near a national park. She had planned to stay at home, meet friends and watch films. On the first day, she felt lonely because the other teenagers seemed confident and loud. But during the first hike, she noticed that several others were nervous too.

On the third day, Mia learned how to make a simple shelter and cook soup over a small fire. She was surprised that she enjoyed working in a team. The nights were cold, and she did not like the insects in the cabin, but she felt proud whenever the group solved a problem together.

When Mia came home, she told her parents that camp had been better than expected. She still missed her friends, but she had learned that trying something unfamiliar could make her braver.

### Questions

1. Why did Mia not want to go to camp?
2. How did she feel on the first day?
3. Name two things Mia learned or did at camp.
4. What did Mia dislike?
5. What did she learn about herself?

## Text 2: A teen internship

Noah joined a short internship at a small technology company. At first, he thought he would only watch adults work, but his team gave him clear instructions and let him test a simple app. He made several mistakes, yet his manager said that asking questions was part of learning.

The company wanted more teenagers to become interested in technology. It also offered special workshops for students who had never written code before. Noah noticed that some students were afraid of not belonging in a tech environment. After one week, he understood that technology was not only about computers. It was also about communication, creativity and solving problems.

### Questions

1. What did Noah expect before the internship?
2. What did he do during the internship?
3. Why did the company offer workshops?
4. Why were some students afraid?
5. What did Noah learn about technology?

## Text 3: Living in California

Lena visited her cousin in Los Angeles. She loved the beaches, the food and the creative atmosphere. However, she also noticed heavy traffic, expensive homes and people sleeping on the streets. Her cousin explained that many families with normal jobs could not afford apartments near their workplace.

After the visit, Lena wrote to a friend that California seemed exciting but unfair. She understood why many people dreamed of living there, but she also saw that a sunny place could have serious social problems.

### Questions

1. What did Lena like about Los Angeles?
2. What problems did she notice?
3. Why can many families not live near work?
4. What is Lena's overall opinion?
5. Which sentence shows a contrast?
